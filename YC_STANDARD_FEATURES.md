**Why YC Cares:** Africa-first, mobile is the primary device

- WhatsApp Business API integration for receipts
- Mobile money integration (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, MTN MoMo)

**Impact:** 10x user adoption potential

**Why YC Cares:** Scalability across African markets

- Daily/weekly business summaries

### 4. **Smart Inventory Predictions (AI/ML)**
**Why YC Cares:** Unique value proposition

- Demand forecasting based on historical data
- Automated reorder suggestions

**Impact:** 30% reduction in stockouts, competitive moat

### 5. **Team/Employee Management**
**Why YC Cares:** B2B expansion, higher ARPU

- Employee performance metrics

**Impact:** 5x ARPU, enterprise clients
