───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
🚀 QUICK START (3 STEPS)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

STEP 1: Create a Promotion
  1. Go to Promotions page
  2. Click "New Promotion"
  3. Fill in form:
     - Name: "Summer Sale"
     - Type: Percentage
     - Value: 20
     - Dates: Today → +7 days
  4. Click "Create"

STEP 2: Apply to a Sale
  1. Go to Sales page
  2. Click "New Sale"
  3. Select product
  4. Choose "Summer Sale" from dropdown
  5. See 20% discount applied automatically!

STEP 3: Record the Sale
  1. Review totals
  2. Click "Record Sale"
  3. Done! ✨
