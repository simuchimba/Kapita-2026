TITLE: Kapita Business Tracking Survey

DESCRIPTION:
Help us build better business tools! This 2-minute survey helps us understand how small businesses track sales, inventory, and finances. Your input shapes our product.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUESTION 1 (Multiple choice - Required)
Title: What do you currently use for business record-keeping?

Options:
○ Pen and paper / Exercise book
○ Excel or Google Sheets
○ WhatsApp or phone notes
○ Accounting software (QuickBooks, Sage, etc.)
○ Point of Sale (POS) system
○ Nothing / I don't track regularly
○ Other

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUESTION 2 (Multiple choice - Required)
Title: What's your BIGGEST challenge with tracking your business?

Options:
○ Too time-consuming
○ Don't know what to track
○ Losing receipts or records
○ Can't see profit clearly
○ Hard to track credit customers
○ Existing tools too expensive
○ Existing tools too complicated
○ Other

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUESTION 3 (Multiple choice - Required)
Title: How often do you record sales and expenses?

Options:
○ Immediately after each transaction
○ At the end of each day
○ Once a week
○ Once a month
○ Rarely or inconsistently

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUESTION 4 (Checkboxes - Required - Max 3 selections)
Title: What business information do you MOST need to see? (Select up to 3)

Options:
☐ Daily/monthly sales totals
☐ Profit margins per product
☐ Low stock alerts
☐ Who owes me money (credits)
☐ Expense breakdown by category
☐ Cash flow status
☐ Top selling products
☐ Other

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUESTION 5 (Multiple choice - Required)
Title: What would you pay MONTHLY for a tool that tracks sales, inventory, customers, profit, and generates reports?

Options:
○ Free only - won't pay
○ Up to K20/month (~$1 USD)
○ K20-50/month (~$1-2 USD)
○ K50-100/month (~$2-4 USD)
○ Over K100/month

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUESTION 6 (Multiple choice - Required)
Title: What type of business do you run?

Options:
○ Retail shop
○ Wholesale
○ Restaurant / Food business
○ Services (salon, repairs, etc.)
○ Market vendor
○ Online business
○ Other

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUESTION 7 (Multiple choice - Required)
Title: How many people work in your business?

Options:
○ Solo (just me)
○ 2-5 people
○ 6-20 people
○ 20+ people

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

QUESTION 8 (Multiple choice - Required)
Title: Would you be interested in early access to test Kapita?

Options:
○ Yes - I want to test the beta version
○ Maybe - Show me more information first
○ No - Not interested right now

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
